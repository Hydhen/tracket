Dragging tickets now offers an "Assign to…" zone next to the edit one, with "Unassign" below it. Dropping on "Assign to…" opens a view with your favorite assignees (up to 5) on top and a search for anyone else below: click a person to assign the tickets to them, and Undo gives them back. Choose your favorites in Settings, under Favorite assignees. "Unassign" is now offered wherever you drag tickets, not only from the Board.
