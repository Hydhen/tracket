Tracket can work with Sprocket, a companion app that runs Claude agent workflows on tickets. Sign in to it in Settings > Sprocket: a ticket then gets an Agents button to start a workflow in its worktree, and an Agents section that follows the run (its steps, the agents at work, the notes they leave each other on the run's board, where you can post too) and lets you approve or reject what its agents ask. Nothing changes until you sign in.
