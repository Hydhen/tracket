A new Appearance page in the settings to choose the theme, with five fun themes besides System, Light and Dark: Terminal (green on black in monospace, with a moving line and CRT edges you can switch off), Neo-brutalist (a cream page with thick black outlines and hard shadows) Mission control (amber on black, with a T+ countdown on every running timer and glitches you can switch off) Quest log (blue windows, white borders and a pixel font, like an old role-playing game) and Neon cyberpunk (cut corners, neon colours and scanlines you can switch off).
