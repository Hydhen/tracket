A new Board tab next to Open, Closed and Tree: explore a Jira board (its current sprint, another sprint, the backlog or the whole board) or any project, and feed your Open list from it. Boards and projects are listed by project name and can be searched. Columns can be folded into a narrow strip, and the board remembers which ones. Drag a ticket onto the Open list, or use "Assign to me" on it, to take it. Tickets that someone else has ask for a confirmation first, and Undo gives them back. Dragging a ticket of the Open list out onto the board unassigns it. The lists change right away and are not reloaded from Jira afterwards, since its search is slow to show the change.
