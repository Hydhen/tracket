A new Board tab next to Open, Closed and Tree: explore a Jira board (its current sprint, another sprint, the backlog or the whole board) or any project, and feed your Open list from it. Drag a ticket onto the Open list, or use "Assign to me" on it, to take it. Tickets that someone else has ask for a confirmation first, and Undo gives them back. Dragging a ticket of the Open list out onto the board unassigns it.
